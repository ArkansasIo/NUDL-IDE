# Noodle Mathematics Curriculum Sequence

## Elementary
Counting, arithmetic, place value, fractions, decimals, ratios, geometry, patterns, logic.

## Middle School
Integers, proportions, equations, coordinates, probability, statistics, transformations, algorithms.

## High School
Algebra, functions, systems, quadratics, trigonometry, analytic geometry, combinatorics, statistics, introductory calculus.

## College
Calculus I-III, linear algebra, discrete mathematics, probability, statistics, numerical methods, data structures, algorithms.

## University
Real analysis, abstract algebra, number theory, differential equations, numerical analysis, optimization, formal logic, computation theory, scientific computing.

## Research
Formal semantics, proof systems, SAT/SMT, symbolic mathematics, compiler optimization, numerical stability, parallel algorithms, verification.
