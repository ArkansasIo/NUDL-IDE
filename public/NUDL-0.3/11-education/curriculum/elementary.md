# Elementary Noodle Mathematics

Beginner: counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, shapes, patterns, logic.

Intermediate: ratios, percentages, coordinate grids, simple statistics, logical statements.

Advanced: multi-step word problems, sequences, introductory combinatorics, computational thinking.

Projects: calculator, fraction calculator, shape perimeter/area, number-pattern generator, truth-table explorer.
